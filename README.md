# Wine Quality EDA

I combined the red and white Wine Quality datasets from UCI to explore how wine chemistry relates to quality ratings. The combined data has 6,497 samples and 13 columns, including a wine type label I added to compare red and white wines.

In the notebook, I check the data, summarize each variable, look at outliers, and use histograms, bar charts, box plots, and scatter plots to explore patterns and relationships.


Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/186/wine+quality
